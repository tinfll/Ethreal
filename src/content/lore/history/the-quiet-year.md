---
title: The Quiet Year
section: history
summary: When a whole year went un-recorded, and what filled the gap.
tags: [history, third-spiral]
order: 1
---

For three hundred and sixty-four days, no chronicler in any of the Six
Cantons wrote a single word. Whether this was conspiracy, plague, or simple
loss of nerve, no one now agrees.

What is recorded is that the year ended, and the world that emerged was
slightly the wrong shape.
