## Origin

Aevris was born during the **Quiet Year**, in a town that no longer appears on
any chart. She remembers it the way one remembers a dream — in textures, not
facts.

## The Compass

The compass she carries is a closed-form solution to a problem nobody has
posed. Its needle obeys:

$$
\vec{n}(t) = \nabla \Phi(\mathbf{x}, t) \quad \text{where} \quad
\Phi(\mathbf{x}, t) = \sum_{k} \omega_k \, e^{-\lambda_k \|\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_k\|^2}
$$

Each $\mathbf{x}_k$ is a place she has *almost* gone; each $\omega_k$ weighs
how badly she didn't.

## Voice

> "A map is a kind of grief. You only draw the edges of what you can't keep."
